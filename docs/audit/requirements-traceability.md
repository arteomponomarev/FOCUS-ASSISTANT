The local CV rows deliberately receive partial evidence when a published artifact is absent. Passing historical executions remain attributed to their exact SHA. See [evidence and scope](test-evidence.md).

Interpretation: PASS means the named requirement has source and/or runtime evidence appropriate to that requirement. PARTIAL shows a real but incomplete delivery or verification. FAIL is a demonstrably absent or unmet requirement. NOT VERIFIED has insufficient evidence. NOT APPLICABLE requires an explicit scope justification through the verification column. P0 is a submission blocker or core deliverable gap, P1 is material, P2 is advanced improvement, P3 is optional. Full-line/branch coverage measurements are separate from grading status. Time estimates assume prerequisites are available. “Grade impact” flags risk and does not assign an automatic penalty.
